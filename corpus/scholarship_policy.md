# Scholarship Policy
## Central Institute of Technology and Sciences

---

## 15. Scholarships

### 15.1 Categories of Scholarship

The Institute offers three categories of scholarship: the Merit Scholarship (based on entrance examination rank), the Need-Based Scholarship (based on documented family income), and the Sports and Cultural Excellence Scholarship (based on achievement at state level or above). A student may hold at most one scholarship category at a time, except that a Sports and Cultural Excellence Scholarship may be held concurrently with a Need-Based Scholarship.

### 15.2 Eligibility Criteria

- Merit Scholarship: awarded on admission based on entrance rank; renewed each year provided the student maintains a CGPA of at least 8.0 and has no backlog courses.
- Need-Based Scholarship: awarded annually on application, subject to income verification; renewed provided the student maintains a CGPA of at least 6.0.
- Sports and Cultural Excellence Scholarship: awarded for the academic year following the qualifying achievement; renewal requires a fresh qualifying achievement in the same academic year.

### 15.3 Scholarship and Academic Probation

A student placed on academic probation shall be ineligible to hold or receive any scholarship for the duration of the probation period. Any scholarship held by the student at the time academic probation is imposed is suspended with immediate effect and no disbursement shall be made for the semester in which probation is imposed or for any subsequent semester in which the student remains on probation. A student who exits probation by meeting the conditions in Section 12.4 of the Academic Regulations may re-apply for a scholarship from the following semester, subject to satisfying the eligibility criteria in Section 15.2 as they then stand.

### 15.4 Scholarship Disbursement

Scholarship amounts are disbursed directly to the student's fee account in two instalments, at the start of each semester, and are applied first against any outstanding tuition fee balance before any residual amount is paid to the student.

### 15.5 Withdrawal of Scholarship for Misconduct

A scholarship held by a student is immediately and permanently withdrawn if the student is found guilty of academic dishonesty under Section 17 (Student Conduct) or is placed on disciplinary probation under Section 18. Amounts already disbursed for the semester in which the finding is made are not required to be refunded, but no further instalment is paid.

### 15.6 Scholarship Continuation After a Failed Semester

This Rulebook does not provide a general mechanism for a student to have scholarship eligibility "restored" after a single failed semester outside of the probation-exit process described in Section 15.3; restoration is governed strictly by whether the student meets the CGPA and backlog criteria in Section 15.2 in the semester following the failure.
