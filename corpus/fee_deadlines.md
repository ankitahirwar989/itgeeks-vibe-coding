# Fee Payment and Deadlines
## Central Institute of Technology and Sciences

---

## 13. Fee Payment

### 13.1 Fee Schedule

The following fees and deadlines apply for each Odd Semester. Even Semester deadlines follow the same schedule shifted by six months (i.e., 15 January instead of 15 July, and so on), unless separately notified by the Registrar.

| Fee Type | Deadline | Late Fee |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Semester Tuition Fee | 15 July | ₹500 per week, up to 4 weeks |
| Examination Fee | 10 November | ₹300 |
| Hostel Fee | 20 July | ₹1,000 |
| Library Security Deposit (first semester only) | 15 July | Not applicable |
| Re-examination Fee (per course) | Within 5 days of result declaration | ₹200 |

### 13.2 Semester Tuition Fee Deadline

The semester tuition fee must be paid on or before 15 July for the Odd Semester and 15 January for the Even Semester. Fees paid after this date will not be accepted through the standard fee portal, and the student's semester registration shall be liable to cancellation for non-payment, subject to the late registration provisions of Section 2.2 of the General Rulebook.

### 13.3 Mode of Payment

Fees may be paid through the online student portal by net banking, debit card, or UPI, or in person at the Accounts Office by demand draft. Cash payments are not accepted for amounts exceeding ₹10,000.

### 13.4 Fee Payment Portal Downtime

The Institute does not guarantee continuous availability of the online fee payment portal. Students are advised to complete payment well in advance of the deadline. The Accounts Office may, at its discretion and on a case-by-case basis, consider evidence of a documented portal outage when assessing a late fee waiver request, but no automatic extension or waiver is granted solely on account of portal downtime.

### 13.5 Refunds

A student who withdraws from the Institute before the start of instruction is entitled to a refund of 90% of the semester tuition fee paid. No refund of the semester tuition fee is issued for withdrawal after the start of instruction. The Library Security Deposit is refundable in full, less any outstanding fines, upon final withdrawal or graduation.

### 13.6 Late Fee Computation

The late fee for the Semester Tuition Fee accrues at ₹500 per week or part thereof, beginning the day after the published deadline, up to a maximum of four weeks. A student who has not paid the Semester Tuition Fee, inclusive of accrued late fee, within four weeks of the published deadline is deemed to have withdrawn from the semester under Section 13.2.

---

## 14. Examination Fee

### 14.1 Payment of Examination Fee

Every student intending to appear for a semester examination must pay the prescribed Examination Fee by 10 November for the Odd Semester (10 April for the Even Semester). A student who has not paid the Examination Fee by this date will not be issued an admit card and will not be permitted to appear for the examination, regardless of attendance eligibility.

### 14.2 Re-examination Fee

A student appearing for a re-examination or backlog examination under Section 10 of the Academic Regulations must pay the Re-examination Fee within five days of the declaration of the result that created the backlog.
