# Central Institute of Technology and Sciences
## General Academic Rulebook

---

## 1. General Provisions

### 1.1 Scope

This Rulebook governs all matters relating to admissions, academic registration, attendance, examinations, grading, scholarships, fees, hostel accommodation, student conduct, and graduation at the Central Institute of Technology and Sciences ("the Institute"). It applies to every student registered in any undergraduate, postgraduate, or diploma programme offered by the Institute, unless a specific programme handbook states otherwise.

### 1.2 Definitions

For the purposes of this Rulebook:

- "Academic Year" means the period from 1 August to 31 July of the following calendar year, divided into two regular semesters (Odd and Even) and an optional Summer Term.
- "Semester" means a term of instruction of approximately sixteen weeks, followed by an examination period.
- "Registrar" means the officer responsible for maintaining academic records, processing registration, and issuing official communications on behalf of the Institute.
- "Academic Committee" means the body constituted under Section 11 of this Rulebook with authority over academic policy exceptions.
- "Department Committee" means the body constituted at the department level under Section 11.4, responsible for first-level academic grievances.
- "Head of Department" (HoD) means the officer in charge of a specific academic department.
- "Controller of Examinations" means the officer responsible for the conduct, security, and result-processing of all semester examinations.

### 1.3 Authority

This Rulebook is approved and amended by the Academic Council of the Institute. In the event of any inconsistency between this Rulebook and a department-level handbook, this Rulebook shall prevail unless the Academic Council has explicitly delegated authority to the department for that specific matter. Any provision of this Rulebook may be varied only by resolution of the Academic Council or, where expressly permitted, by the Academic Committee acting under its delegated exceptional powers described in Section 11.1.

## 2. Academic Registration

### 2.1 Semester Registration

Every student must complete semester registration, including academic advising sign-off and payment of the semester fee, within the registration window published by the Registrar for each semester, which typically opens four weeks before the semester begins and closes on the first day of instruction.

### 2.2 Late Registration

A student who fails to register within the standard registration window may complete late registration, including payment of the semester fee, at any time up to 31 July for the Odd Semester and up to 31 December for the Even Semester, upon payment of a late registration fee of ₹500. Registration completed within this late window shall be treated as valid and shall not be cancelled solely on account of its lateness. A student who has not registered, including payment of fee, by the applicable late-registration cut-off date shall be deemed to have withdrawn from the semester and must apply afresh for readmission.

### 2.3 Registration Holds

The Registrar may place a registration hold on a student's record for outstanding library fines, unreturned equipment, or a pending disciplinary matter. A hold must be cleared before the student can complete registration for the subsequent semester.

## 3. Semester Enrollment and Course Selection

### 3.1 Course Load

A full-time student must enroll in a minimum of 15 and a maximum of 24 credits per regular semester. Enrollment below the minimum requires written approval from the Head of Department, ordinarily granted only in the student's final semester or on medical grounds.

### 3.2 Add/Drop Period

Students may add or drop courses without academic or financial penalty during the first two weeks of instruction. After this period, changes to enrollment are governed by Section 3.3 (Course Withdrawal).

### 3.3 Course Withdrawal

A student may withdraw from an individual course after the add/drop period and up to the end of the tenth week of instruction by submitting a withdrawal form countersigned by the course instructor and the Head of Department. A withdrawn course is recorded on the transcript with the grade "W" and is not counted in the computation of the semester grade point average. No fee refund is issued for a withdrawn course. Withdrawal after the tenth week of instruction is not permitted except by decision of the Academic Committee under its exceptional powers.

## 4. Identity Cards

### 4.1 Issuance

Every registered student is issued an Institute Identity Card at the start of their first semester. The card must be carried at all times on campus and must be presented for entry to examinations, the library, and hostel premises.

### 4.2 Loss or Damage

A student whose identity card is lost, stolen, or damaged must report the matter to the Registrar's office within three working days and apply for a duplicate card upon payment of a replacement fee of ₹200. A duplicate card is normally issued within five working days of the request.

---

## 5. Library Rules

### 5.1 Borrowing Privileges

Undergraduate students may borrow up to five books at a time for a loan period of fourteen days; postgraduate students may borrow up to eight books for a loan period of twenty-one days. Loans may be renewed once, provided no other reservation exists on the title.

### 5.2 Overdue Fines

A fine of ₹5 per book per day is charged for overdue items, up to a maximum of ₹500 per book, beyond which the item is deemed lost and charged at replacement cost.

### 5.3 Reference and Reserved Materials

Materials designated "Reference Only" or placed on "Reserve" for a specific course may not be borrowed and must be used within the library premises.

## 6. Administrative Procedures

### 6.1 Official Communication

All official communication from the Institute to a student is sent to the student's Institute-issued email address. A student is deemed to have received a communication three working days after it is sent to this address, regardless of whether it was actually read.

### 6.2 Change of Personal Details

A student must notify the Registrar in writing within fifteen days of any change to their legal name, permanent address, or emergency contact details, accompanied by supporting documentation where the change relates to legal name.

### 6.3 Record Retention

The Institute retains academic records permanently and retains administrative records, including fee receipts and hostel allotment records, for a minimum of seven years from the date of the student's graduation or withdrawal.

### 6.4 Transcripts and Certificates

A student or alumnus may request an official transcript or provisional certificate from the Registrar's office upon payment of the prescribed fee. Transcripts are ordinarily issued within seven working days, provided the student's fee account shows no outstanding balance.

## 20. Admissions

### 20.1 Admission Categories

The Institute admits students to undergraduate and postgraduate programmes under the following categories: General Category (based on rank in the Institute's own Common Entrance Test, "CET"), Management Quota (up to 15% of sanctioned seats per programme, subject to the same minimum eligibility marks as the General Category), Lateral Entry (for diploma holders seeking direct admission to the second year of an undergraduate programme, subject to availability of vacant seats), and International Student Category (subject to a separate application process administered by the International Students Office).

### 20.2 Minimum Eligibility

An applicant for undergraduate admission must have passed the qualifying higher secondary examination with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (45% for candidates belonging to a reserved category recognised under applicable law). An applicant for postgraduate admission must hold a relevant undergraduate degree with a minimum CGPA of 6.0 or equivalent aggregate percentage.

### 20.3 Entrance Examination and Counselling

Admission to the General Category and Management Quota is based on rank in the CET, followed by a counselling process conducted by the Admissions Office in which applicants are called in order of rank to select a programme from the seats available at that time. A rank-holder who does not report for counselling on their allotted date and time, or within any extended window separately notified for that round, forfeits the seat offered in that round but may participate in a subsequent round if seats remain.

### 20.4 Provisional Admission and Document Verification

Admission is provisional until the Admissions Office verifies original certificates of educational qualification, category certificates (where applicable), and identity documents. An admission is cancelled without refund of the admission processing fee if verification reveals that a certificate submitted by the applicant is forged or materially misrepresents the applicant's qualifications.

### 20.5 Seat Cancellation and Refund on Withdrawal Before Instruction Begins

An admitted student who withdraws before the start of instruction is entitled to a refund as described in Section 13.5 of the Fee Payment policy. A seat vacated in this manner is offered to the next eligible applicant on the waiting list for that category and programme.

### 20.6 Migration Between Programmes

A student admitted to one undergraduate programme may apply to migrate to a different undergraduate programme within the Institute only at the end of the first year, subject to (a) availability of a vacant seat in the target programme, (b) the applicant's original CET rank meeting the closing rank for the target programme in the applicant's admission year, and (c) approval of the Academic Committee. Migration requests after the first year are not considered.
