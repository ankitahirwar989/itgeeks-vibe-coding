# Attendance Policy
## Central Institute of Technology and Sciences

---

## 3. Attendance

### 3.1 Minimum Attendance

Students must maintain at least 75% attendance in each registered course to be eligible to appear for the semester examination in that course. Attendance is calculated separately for every course a student is registered in; meeting the requirement in one course does not offset a shortfall in another. Under no circumstances shall a student whose attendance in a course falls below 75% be permitted to appear for the semester examination in that course, and no departmental waiver of this specific threshold may be granted at the department level.

### 3.2 Attendance Calculation

Attendance percentage for a course is calculated as:

    (Number of classes attended / Number of classes held) × 100

Classes held include lectures, tutorials, and laboratory sessions as scheduled on the official timetable for that course. A student who joins a course after the add/drop period has their attendance calculated only from the date of joining. Attendance in a class that is officially cancelled by the instructor (as opposed to a class the student misses) is excluded from both the numerator and the denominator.

### 3.3 Attendance for Laboratory Courses

Laboratory sessions are counted individually in the attendance calculation on the same basis as lecture sessions; there is no separate or reduced attendance threshold for laboratory-only courses.

### 3.4 Condonation of Shortfall

A student whose attendance in a course falls between 65% and 75% may apply to the Head of Department for condonation of the shortfall, supported by a written explanation. The Head of Department may condone up to 10 percentage points of shortfall per course per semester, restoring eligibility to appear for the examination in that course. No more than two courses per semester may receive condonation under this clause, and a student may not receive condonation in the same course in two consecutive semesters.

### 3.5 Attendance Records and Grievances

Attendance is recorded by the course instructor and published to students at least once every four weeks during the semester. A student who disputes their recorded attendance must raise the matter with the course instructor within seven days of publication. Unresolved disputes are referred to the Department Committee described in Section 11.4.

## 7. Leave of Absence

### 7.1 Short Leave

A student may apply for short leave of up to seven consecutive days per semester for personal reasons, subject to approval by the course instructors of the courses affected. Short leave does not exempt the student from the attendance calculation described in Section 3.2; missed classes during approved short leave are still counted as classes not attended for the purpose of the 75% threshold, unless the absence separately qualifies for a medical exemption under Section 9.

### 7.2 Extended Leave of Absence

A student requiring leave of more than two weeks for documented medical, family bereavement, or legal reasons may apply to the Head of Department for an Extended Leave of Absence. If granted, the affected period is excluded entirely from the attendance denominator for the courses concerned, provided the application is submitted, with supporting documents, within ten days of the start of the leave period.

### 7.3 Leave for Institute-Sanctioned Activities

A student representing the Institute in a sporting, cultural, or academic event that has been formally sanctioned and listed by the Dean of Student Affairs is treated as present for attendance purposes for the days of official participation and authorised travel, provided the event is on the Dean's published list of sanctioned activities for that academic year. Participation in an event not on this published list is not covered by this clause and is treated as ordinary absence for attendance purposes.
