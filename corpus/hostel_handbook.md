# Hostel Handbook
## Central Institute of Technology and Sciences

---

## 16. Hostel Rules

### 16.1 Eligibility for Hostel Accommodation

Hostel accommodation is available to all registered full-time students on a first-come, first-served basis, subject to availability. Priority is given to first-year students and students residing more than 50 km from the campus.

### 16.2 Hostel Fee

The Hostel Fee, as set out in the fee table in Section 13.1 of the Fee Payment policy, must be paid by 20 July for the Odd Semester and 20 January for the Even Semester. Hostel accommodation is provisional until the Hostel Fee is paid in full.

### 16.3 Hostel Fee Refund

A student who vacates hostel accommodation before the end of a semester for reasons other than graduation or Institute-approved withdrawal is not entitled to a refund of the Hostel Fee already paid for that semester, irrespective of the number of days of early checkout, except where the vacancy is created by disciplinary expulsion initiated by the Institute, in which case the matter is decided by the Dean of Student Affairs on a case-by-case basis.

### 16.4 Room Allotment

Room allotment is made by the Hostel Warden at the start of each academic year. A request for a room change during the year may be made in writing to the Warden and is granted subject to availability and the consent of the other occupants of the requested room.

### 16.5 Curfew and Night Leave

Hostel gates close at 10:30 PM on weekdays and 11:30 PM on weekends. A resident wishing to remain outside the hostel beyond these times must obtain Night Leave from the Warden in advance, recorded in the Night Leave register.

### 16.6 Guests and Visitors

Visitors are permitted in the common areas of the hostel between 9:00 AM and 8:00 PM. Overnight guests are not permitted under any circumstances.

### 16.7 Prohibited Items

Cooking appliances, except electric kettles rated below 1000W, and any form of open flame are prohibited in hostel rooms. Possession of alcohol, tobacco, or any intoxicating substance on hostel premises is prohibited and is treated as a disciplinary matter under Section 18.

### 16.8 Hostel Disciplinary Action

A resident found in breach of hostel rules is subject to disciplinary action by the Warden, ranging from a written warning to expulsion from the hostel, in accordance with the severity provisions of Section 18.4 (Disciplinary Procedures). Serious breaches, including those involving prohibited items under Section 16.7 or breach of curfew on more than three occasions in a semester, are referred to the Hostel Disciplinary Committee.

### 16.9 Appeals Against Hostel Disciplinary Decisions

A resident may appeal a decision of the Warden or the Hostel Disciplinary Committee to the Dean of Student Affairs within thirty days of the decision being communicated. The Dean's decision on such an appeal is final. This Handbook does not provide for any appeal, extension, or exception to this thirty-day window; a resident who does not file within thirty days has no further recourse under this Handbook.

### 16.10 Maintenance and Damage

Residents are collectively liable for damage to common hostel property in their wing where the individual responsible cannot be identified. Damage to a resident's own room is charged to that resident individually at cost, as assessed by the Estate Office.

### 16.11 Mess and Dining Facilities

Hostel residents are automatically enrolled in the Institute mess facility, and the mess fee is billed separately from the Hostel Fee on a monthly basis in arrears, based on actual attendance recorded by biometric scan at the dining hall entrance. A resident travelling on approved Institute business or Extended Leave of Absence under Section 7.2 of the Attendance Policy may apply to the Mess Committee for a pro-rata mess fee rebate for the period of absence, provided the absence is at least five consecutive days and is notified to the Mess Committee in advance.

### 16.12 Day Scholars and Mess Access

A student who does not reside in the hostel ("Day Scholar") may apply to the Mess Committee for a Day Scholar Meal Card, permitting access to the mess facility on a per-meal payment basis. A Day Scholar Meal Card does not entitle the holder to any hostel accommodation privilege and is granted subject to seating capacity at the discretion of the Mess Committee.

### 16.13 Medical Grounds for Room Preference

A resident with a documented medical condition may request the Warden to give preference to a ground-floor room or a room near hostel medical facilities when allotting or reallotting rooms. The Warden considers such requests alongside other allotment criteria in Section 16.4 but is not obligated to guarantee a specific room, since final allotment remains subject to availability.

### 16.14 Grievance Redress Within the Hostel

A resident with a grievance concerning mess quality, maintenance, or day-to-day hostel administration (as distinct from a disciplinary matter under Section 16.8) may raise the matter with the Hostel Residents' Council, which meets monthly with the Warden. The Council's recommendations are advisory and are not binding on the Warden or the Estate Office.
